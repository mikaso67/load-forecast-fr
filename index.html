Methods & Modeling Architecture

I developed this forecasting system to deliver French national day-ahead electricity consumption predictions at 30-minute resolution. Every forecast is frozen before the 12:00 Europe/Paris cutoff on D-1, strictly respecting forecast-time boundaries without retrospective leakage. The production point forecast aggregates four expert models (per-slot linear regression, per-slot GAM with penalized B-splines, per-slot GAM with online Kalman filter, and global LightGBM) using online ML-Poly weighting updated daily with D-2 losses. Prediction intervals use 80% Mondrian seasonal split-conformal inference over a 365-day trailing pool. Production serving uses real-time lags mapped to consolidated scale (variant B) with variant A in parallel shadow. Operational quality is continuously tracked using a two-sided CUSUM on standardized seasonal residuals (h = 11.46, k = 0.5) alongside rolling 28-day skill evaluations against RTE D-1.
